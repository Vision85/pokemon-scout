THE SCOUT METHOD

Four checks. One considered decision.

01 / Completed sales

Use comparable transactions, not active listings. Keep a source link, sale date, currency, and shipping treatment.

02 / Condition

Compare like for like. A raw card and a graded copy belong in separate research records.

03 / Supply & reprints

Check official announcements. Distinguish confirmed releases from speculation.

04 / Exit & budget

Record your maximum spend and consider fees, shipping, time, and potential losses.
